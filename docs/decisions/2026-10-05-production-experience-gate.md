## What was trialed

Two disposable instruments were committed and observed on the primary device, a Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.

SPATIAL-PROTOTYPE-001 is `252b12af754278c44abbe9644e5517447d3fbdf4`. SPATIAL-PROTOTYPE-002 is `1475c928903e9284e66ed4609b159188cd1b5dd4`. Both live at `/instrument`. Both remain experimental spatial evidence. Their source choices are not canon because they exist in the repository.

The trials used one temporal field, a secondary reach region, and a Direction companion only while asking Month. They reused existing domain reads and writes. Present, Day, Week, and Month were questions of one loaded reality. Context focus was a transient lens. Explicit Save remained the write. Co-occupation, typed silence, and an Active Thread off the clock remained in force.

The second trial reduced the resting reach region, replaced four standing question buttons with a transient question control, compacted Context focus and Capture, moved verbose fact language toward inspection, quieted the present mark, and moved Sign out behind a small Account disclosure. Those mechanisms were hypotheses under test. This record does not adopt them.

Screenshots from either trial are not a visual specification.

## Human evidence

The physical trials established these findings. They established relationships and spatial authority.

### A. One temporal reality can support multiple questions

Present, Day, Week, and Month can operate as different questions of the same temporal reality. They do not require separate applications. This finding does not canonize navigation or transition mechanics.

### B. The temporal field is primary

The resting phone experience should be dominated by temporal territory, not by controls about that territory.

### C. Secondary capabilities need reach, not permanent expansion

Active Thread, question change, Context focus, Capture, inspection, and low-frequency management do not all require simultaneous expanded screen territory.

### D. Intention requires persistence

The Active Thread belongs within immediate orientation and recovery reach. Its absence may be quieter than an established thread. Absence stays visible. The thread is not a recommendation, a priority, or a next action.

### E. Context is a lens

Context focus can change emphasis while facts remain in the same temporal reality. It must not become workspace navigation, an application mode, or a persisted current Context.

### F. Capture may temporarily expand

Capture must remain immediately reachable. It need not permanently consume large temporal territory. Invoking it may temporarily occupy more secondary space while the human's place in time stays put.

### G. The field may speak spatially

Temporal facts can communicate substantially through position, bounds, duration, semantic kind, and co-occupation. Verbose metadata need not be permanently painted into temporal territory when inspection can recover it. This finding does not authorize hiding meaning or reducing accessible names.

### H. Inspection may speak verbally

Reference and inspection can carry richer labels, exact bounds, purpose, associations, and other detail. That language does not all have to occupy the field.

### I. Now is locative

The present can be a location inside the temporal field. It does not become another dashboard or a recommendation surface.

### J. Week can be perceived as shape

Week can be compressed perception of established temporal structure. It is not seven independent editable Day surfaces. The trials do not support workload, density, utilization, busyness, success, or availability.

### K. Month can hold temporal landscape beside Direction

Broader temporal structure and retained Direction can coexist. Direction is not assigned to dates. Coexistence does not show progress, expression, trajectory, alignment, or causality.

### L. Co-occupation remains valid

Multiple temporal truths can occupy the same minutes. Coextensive occupancy does not require coextensive verbose labeling. Shared territory must still expose every relevant fact.

### M. Device-specific expression remains valid

Phone and desktop are expressions of the same ontology. They are not required to be one layout stretched across widths.

## Production experience hierarchy

The trials support this hierarchy for production design. It stays subordinate to accessibility, epistemic integrity, and semantic truth.

**Time gets territory.** The temporal field receives primary spatial authority because time is the canvas of Orient.

**Intention gets persistence.** The Active Thread survives interruption and remains quickly recoverable. It is not promoted into recommendation, priority, or next action.

**Controls get reach.** Controls required to operate the instrument remain discoverable and efficient. They do not compete spatially with the reality being manipulated.

**Detail gets inspection.** Information recoverable from spatial position, or requested through reference, does not permanently occupy temporal territory.

## Production experience design

PRODUCTION-EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-001 is authorized.

It derives the actual production experience from canonical semantics, the encounter model, the physical spatial evidence in this record, device realities, accessibility, interaction requirements, and the product's identity.

It resolves the production composition sufficiently to implement. It is not another prototype, not a disposable route, and not a cosmetic skin.

It addresses, at minimum:

**Phone.** Overall spatial composition. Temporal field boundaries. Movement through time. Changing temporal question and resolution. Present. Active Thread. Context focus. Capture. Fact reference and inspection. Temporal establishment. Week. Month. Direction. Transient working surfaces. Account and other low-frequency management. Keyboard behavior. One-handed reach. Spatial continuity.

**Desktop.** Larger temporal territory. The relationship between the field and secondary tools. Month beside Direction. How additional width is used without becoming a dashboard. Interaction equivalence without requiring layout identity.

**Visual language.** Typography. Semantic color. Material. Transparency or translucency only where semantically earned. Overlap and co-occupation. The present instant. The relationship of past, present, and future. Typed silence. Selection, reference, and establishment states. Direction's distinct material language. Context-focus emphasis. Accessibility and contrast.

**Motion.** Passage of time. Question and resolution transition. Establishment. Bound adjustment. Withdrawal. Focus change. Capture invocation. Return and findability. Motion reports a real change. It does not decorate the interface.

Prior visual vision may be evidence. Concept images are not a specification. A predetermined glass aesthetic is not required. Orient's semantics continue to determine its form.

## Unresolved production question

Reducing bottom control gravity made the top composition newly visible as unresolved.

The prototype still places a question label, a civil date, Earlier, and Later above the temporal field. Together with the lower controls, the phone can read as a header, navigation, time, and controls, rather than as one continuous temporal instrument.

Production design must resolve how the human moves through time and changes temporal resolution as part of the instrument itself.

This record does not solve that question. It does not canonize swipe, pinch, a scrubber, buttons, arrows, tabs, a wheel, or any other mechanism. PRODUCTION-EXPERIENCE-DESIGN-001 carries the question.

## Production UI

Production UI is the actual Orient experience intended to replace the scaffold.

It is not another `/prototype-*` route, another disposable instrument, a cosmetic skin over the current scaffold, a design-system exercise detached from interaction, or a static mockup with no relationship to canonical runtime truth.

The production experience must eventually be implemented against real Orient data and existing deterministic capabilities. This record does not implement it. Operational adoption stays open.
