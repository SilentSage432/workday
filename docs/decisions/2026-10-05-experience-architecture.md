# EXPERIENCE-ARCHITECTURE-001 — One field of time

Date: 2026-10-05.

Baseline: `2ce557113b687e882d87c4129892582865bfdff7`.

Discovery: EXPERIENCE-EMERGENCE-001. Decision A is accepted. Enough evidence exists to define Experience Architecture. This record canonizes how the human encounters Orient. It does not choose a layout, a component, a palette, a typeface, a material, an animation, a navigation control, or a prototype. It does not implement a surface.

## Decision

Accepted.

The human operates a field of time they can point at.

Established temporal truths occupy that field without canceling each other. The human can refer to bare temporal territory and explicitly establish new truth there. Changing resolution changes the question asked of the same reality. Direction remains beside temporal structure. The present instant remains locatable without becoming authoritative. An explicitly retained thread of intention remains recoverable when the lived moment and that intention come apart.

## Experience identity

Orient is experientially one temporal instrument over one human reality.

The human is not operating a task manager, a calendar joined to a task list, a productivity dashboard, a project-management system, separate Work / Family / TeamLab / Financial workspaces, an AI assistant, or four unrelated Present / Day / Week / Month applications.

Familiar interaction language is welcome where it truthfully maps to this ontology. Spatial architecture follows the ontology.

## Time is the field

Time is the primary experiential field. Temporal territory receives the majority of meaningful spatial authority.

Truths that possess temporal territory belong on that field:

- a Work shift
- Protected Time
- a Block
- a Commitment

Truths without temporal coordinates are not assigned a clock position in order to complete the picture:

- Destination
- Priority
- Note
- Active Thread
- an ordinary Task
- `planned_on`
- Due
- Must Do

Controls, Capture, inspection, and management are secondary to the field. They must not casually displace the temporal territory currently under the human's hand.

## One reality, several questions

Present, Day, Week, and Month are different questions asked of the same temporal reality.

They are not separate products, independent temporal databases, four conventional screens, or a pure continuous zoom. The experience preserves continuity of that reality while the admitted information and the interaction authority change with the question.

| Resolution | Experiential job |
| --- | --- |
| Present | Orient inside the lived instant. Perceive which established temporal truths contain now. Recover the explicitly retained Active Thread. |
| Day | Perceive exact temporal territory. Refer to time, refine bounds, establish temporal meaning, and inspect or manipulate established temporal truth. |
| Week | Perceive the located shape of established temporal structure across a bounded span. Preserve kind and overlap. Perceive where no participating temporal structure is established. Do not infer availability, workload, density, utilization, or success. |
| Month | Perceive retained Direction beside broader temporal structure. Keep Destination and Priority non-temporal. Preserve explicit execution-to-Priority relationships without turning them into progress. Month is not a longer Week. |

The semantic roles remain [2026-10-04-present-moment-orientation.md](2026-10-04-present-moment-orientation.md), [2026-10-02-day-canvas.md](2026-10-02-day-canvas.md), [2026-10-05-week-contract.md](2026-10-05-week-contract.md), and [2026-10-05-month-contract.md](2026-10-05-month-contract.md). This record does not redefine those questions.

## Resolution transformation

Changing resolution changes the question, not the underlying reality.

Perceptual continuity is preserved where it can be preserved without implying that Month is Week zoomed out or that Present is Day zoomed in.

A transition may alter which truths are admitted, how much temporal precision is perceptible, which interaction authority exists, and which non-temporal material becomes visible.

This record does not specify the control or gesture that changes resolution. It does not canonize a zoom slider or Day / Week / Month tabs.

## Day

Day is the exact manipulable temporal field.

A familiar vertical local clock is permitted. Temporal position maps to local clock position, and direct vertical selection survived embodied phone use. That familiar form does not grant conventional calendar semantics.

Day preserves:

- the civil clock, rather than an assumed workday crop
- direct temporal reference
- selection through already established paint
- legal overlap
- semantic kind
- explicit establishment
- the location of the present instant
- all-day truth outside timed territory
- uncertainty, rather than a guessed placement, when a local time cannot be resolved

Work is not a selectable meaning for an arbitrary span. A Task is not a selectable meaning. Giving time to a Task remains a Block that may cite that Task. The decision is [2026-10-05-task-time-contract.md](2026-10-05-task-time-contract.md).

## Week

Week is perception of temporal shape.

Week makes perceptible where established temporal structure exists, which semantic kind exists there, where kinds co-occupy the same territory, and where no participating temporal structure is established.

Week does not become seven editable Day canvases, an event-title mini-calendar, a density map, a utilization heatmap, a workload visualization, a weekly Capacity total, or generic busy/free.

A sparse Week is not good. A dense Week is not bad. A large Block is not more important because it is large.

Interaction beyond perception remains unresolved until a later decision earns it. [2026-10-05-week-contract.md](2026-10-05-week-contract.md) remains the semantic authority. How a life-wide Week span is chosen remains open.

## Month

Month is two materials perceived together: retained Direction, and broader temporal structure.

Direction here is a Destination, a Priority, and the explicit retained relationships already authorized by [2026-10-05-direction-contract.md](2026-10-05-direction-contract.md) and [2026-10-05-execution-direction-contract.md](2026-10-05-execution-direction-contract.md).

Temporal structure is the participating kinds Month may already compose. Kinds stay distinct. Nothing established is not availability.

Direction has no temporal coordinates. Destination is not placed on a date. Priority is not placed on a date. Temporal activity is not drawn as progress. Temporal density is not Direction health. Coexistence is not service. An execution-to-Destination edge is not manufactured. A Task or a Block in service of a Priority, and that Priority downstream of a Destination, remain two relationships.

The spatial relationship current evidence supports is: Direction sits beside temporal structure.

"Beside" is not behind, not above as a score, not a horizon, not an axis, not a coordinate system, and not a trajectory. The exact composition of that adjacency is a later design decision. How a Month span is chosen remains open.

## The present instant

The present instant earns locative prominence because the human currently exists there. It does not earn decision authority.

The eventual experience may keep the instant continuously findable, visibly moving with actual time, and perceptible through overlapping truths.

It does not choose a containing fact, elect a current Context, infer importance, imply urgency, determine a next action, or enlarge one truth because that truth contains now. Several truths may contain the same instant. The mark passes through them.

The present locates. It does not decide.

The composition remains Current Temporal Orientation and the Active Thread. They stay independent. The experience does not have to be named NOW. [2026-10-04-present-moment-orientation.md](2026-10-04-present-moment-orientation.md) remains the composition authority. This record does not add inputs to that composition.

## Active Thread

The Active Thread is the human's explicitly retained intention, off the clock.

The experience lets the human recover "I got pulled away. Where was I?" quickly. A useful offer may name that thread, as Resume already does. It does not recite a backlog or choose a next action.

The thread stays independent of temporal containment, the current instant, Context, a Work shift, a Block, a Commitment, and any inferred next action. The present instant and the Active Thread may disagree. That disagreement is ordinary evidence. It is not an error.

The thread does not become a giant banner, an urgency treatment, a modal state, or an inferred recommendation. V0 retains one thread. This record does not invent a suspended thread per Context.

## Co-occupation

Co-occupation is the overlap grammar.

Multiple truths may occupy the same temporal territory. Overlap does not imply conflict, a winner, replacement, rank, or a double-booking judgment. The experience preserves semantic identity, temporal location, and access to the underlying temporal territory.

Established temporal truth must not make the territory underneath it unreachable.

Lane packing is not canonized as the visual solution. Glass is not canonized as the material solution.

## Transparency and depth

Transparency is a candidate technique where one truth must not erase another. Transparency is not itself semantic canon. Glass is not canonical.

Depth does not mean a Direction relationship. Z-order does not mean importance. Coexistence is not service.

A later visual design may use translucency, contour, depth, material, stacking, or another technique only where the treatment truthfully serves this grammar.

## Color

Color may communicate semantic identity.

Color does not silently communicate importance, urgency, Priority rank, progress, success or failure, performance, or conflict.

Temporal distinctions that deserve a persistent identity include a Work shift, Protected Time, a Block, a Commitment, a transient temporal reference, and the present instant as a locative mark.

Context is not a kind of time. This record does not establish a palette.

## Visual weight

Visual weight may help communicate semantic type, including the difference between a field condition and a bounded temporal object.

Visual weight does not communicate importance. Duration already influences spatial size and does not become importance.

This record does not imply that a Commitment outweighs a Block, that Protected Time outweighs chosen time, that longer is more important, or that a Priority with more execution is more important. Obligated time is not thereby more important. Chosen time is not thereby optional.

Must Do may remain prominent on the Task because the human set that attention flag. Must Do stays off the temporal field. It is not Priority.

## Motion

Motion reports a real change.

Earned candidates:

- passage of time, so the present mark moves
- a temporal-bound adjustment, so the boundary moves
- establishment, so a transient reference becomes durable truth
- withdrawal, so the established mark leaves and the territory remains
- Context focus, so emphasis changes on the same reality
- resolution change, so the admitted question changes while continuity is preserved

Decorative futuristic motion is not earned. Motion does not imply urgency, a recommendation, importance, "coming next," or system intelligence.

The field must not move merely because a control opened. Spatial stability is an interaction requirement. Something may still move when the meaning of the surface requires it.

## Typed silence

Silence is typed. The experience keeps these distinct:

| Silence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing established | No participating fact occupies the territory. No availability claim follows. |
| Unavailable for allocation | Protected Time. The territory is unavailable. An activity inside it is not required. Protected does not mean occupied. |
| Available inside an established allocatable boundary | Only where a valid Capacity boundary exists may uncovered territory carry availability meaning. A scheduled Work shift may bound a Work reading and is not itself Capacity. |
| Incomplete evidence | Orient cannot truthfully complete the reading. This is never rendered as empty reality. |

Work Off is not free time. Missing Work schedule evidence is not an empty life. This record does not specify the visual treatment of any silence. The Capacity meaning remains [2026-10-05-capacity-contract.md](2026-10-05-capacity-contract.md).

## Context as a lens

Context focus changes emphasis on one reality. It does not establish a current Context, switch a workspace, switch a profile, create another timeline, or rewrite facts.

"Show me Work" focuses the experience. "Show me everything" lifts that focus. There is no current Context. The decision is [2026-10-05-multi-context-contract.md](2026-10-05-multi-context-contract.md).

Current evidence supports changing emphasis on Context-bearing facts and on facts that already exist only as Work. Context-neutral truths are not silently assigned to a Context, and they are not erased merely because they are neutral. The exact spatial treatment of those neutral truths under a lens remains open. This record does not invent a filtering semantics.

## Direction as an adjacent field

At Month resolution, Direction is retained off-clock material perceived beside temporal structure.

Destination and Priority remain human-established meaning. Their relationships remain inspectable while the human retains them. Withdrawal removes an execution-to-Priority relationship. The experience does not keep it as a ghost.

Direction is not expressed with date coordinates, distance remaining, brightness as progress, a progress bar, an execution count, temporal density, or a trajectory.

A Task or a Block without directional ancestry remains complete and ordinary. Optional ancestry is not a quality score.

## Reference and establishment

Reference is not establishment.

The human may point at time, select an established truth, refine bounds, and fill a draft without changing canonical truth. Explicit establishment is a separate authority boundary.

Where a temporal fact is established from a selected span, the accepted sequence remains:

refer to time → refine bounds → name the semantic meaning → provide the meaning-specific information → explicitly establish the truth.

Not every operation requires every step. Quick Capture, general Capture, the Active Thread, a Work week, and a directional relationship keep the establishment paths that already own them.

## Save

Explicit Save currently marks the transition from draft or reference to established truth for the accepted editable facts: Protected Time, a Block, a Commitment, an open Task's established fields, and a Work week.

This record does not replace Save with implicit persistence. Auto-save would collapse reference into establishment and would need its own later semantic evidence. Cancel, Close, or leaving a draft does not silently establish truth. Delete stays explicit and confirmed where that confirmation is already accepted. Undo remains unresolved.

## Durable touch grammar

Accepted interaction evidence:

- A short tap on an established temporal fact refers to that truth.
- A hold, then a drag, refers to temporal territory, including through established paint.
- Movement before the hold settles is navigation or scroll.
- A temporal selection is transient. It is not a fact, and it is not an availability claim.
- Direct manipulation precedes precision controls.
- Precision controls exist for accuracy and do not dominate the interaction.
- Snapping to 15 minutes is a convenience, not temporal ontology.
- Stored temporal precision stays finer than that gesture convenience.
- Established paint does not seal the underlying territory.

This record does not canonize scaffold constants: hold duration, movement slop, scrollport height, lane geometry, z-index, or card size. Edge handles remain a candidate refinement, not yet canon. The evidence is [2026-10-02-direct-time-selection.md](2026-10-02-direct-time-selection.md) and the later day-canvas tranches. Those thresholds stay implementation details.

## Established-fact lifecycle

The accepted lifecycle is create, see, select, inspect, edit, save, delete.

Selection and inspection are references. Editing manipulates a draft. Save establishes the edited truth.

This record does not invent edit, delete, or archive semantics for a Note. Those remain unresolved. Reference to a Note establishes nothing.

## Capture

Capture stays within reach of the temporal instrument and does not become the instrument.

Quick Capture establishes a Task directly from a title and an explicit Save. It does not infer time, Context, Due, or Must Do, and it does not start the Active Thread.

General Capture retains a human expression. One explicit act may establish a Note, a Task, or nothing. Voice on the primary phone may continue through the device keyboard's dictation into that expression.

Making Capture's controls visible must not displace the temporal field. The phone evidence that opening retained experience moved the surrounding field is the reason for this rule. It does not choose a drawer, a sheet, or a destination.

## Notes

A Note is retained experience, reachable through Capture's memory.

A Note is not an unfinished Task, a temporal interval, a generic notes workspace, or mandatory future work. Notes stay off the temporal field. Selecting a Note refers to it. Establishing a Task from a Note is a separate act. The Note remains. This record does not invent Note edit, delete, or archive.

## Phone

The phone is the moving expression of the temporal instrument. Primary device evidence centers on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.

The phone prioritizes continuity while moving, present orientation, the lived temporal slice, direct touch manipulation, capture in seconds, interruption recovery, one-handed access to frequent acts, and spatial stability.

The phone is not a shrunken dashboard. A control must not casually relocate the temporal material the human was operating. A control that is semantically correct and spatially undiscoverable fails this requirement.

## Desktop

Desktop is the larger spatial expression of the same instrument.

Additional space primarily lets more of the same reality be perceived at once: broader temporal territory, Direction beside temporal structure, overlap without a management surface covering the field, and precise manipulation with more room.

Desktop does not become a dashboard because more pixels exist. The current `max-w-lg` column is scaffold behavior, not desktop architecture.

## Phone and desktop

The relationship is one instrument with device-specific spatial affordances.

Phone and desktop share the ontology, the temporal field, the questions, the establishment boundary, and the authority rules. They may differ in how much territory is perceptible at once, in hand and pointer affordances, and in the spatial composition the device can hold.

This record does not authorize two products. It does not authorize stretching the current phone scaffold to fill a large display.

## Familiarity

Comprehensibility outranks novelty.

Familiar language that truthfully maps to Orient includes a vertical local clock, direct drag, selection, explicit Save, inspection, secondary precision controls, and direct edge refinement if a later decision earns it.

Patterns that import a false ontology stay out: event CRUD as the product, a task-dashboard home, high / medium / low Priority, generic free/busy, workspace switching, goal-progress visualization, utilization heatmaps, and an inferred "Coming Next."

Orient may look recognizable. It does not behave like a conventional calendar.

## Scaffold

The existing scaffold is an engineering instrument that produced accepted interaction evidence. Its durable interaction truths stay. Its spatial architecture is not the target merely because the code exists.

These are not target architecture:

- Tasks and Schedule as peer product destinations
- a narrow phone column on desktop
- management forms as the primary spatial structure
- bottom navigation as canonical identity
- present orientation detached from the temporal field
- Capture expansion that displaces the field
- an establishment panel that hides the next required interaction

Do not polish the scaffold into the product. A later implementation may replace it. This record does not replace it.

## Principles

1. Time is the field. Meaningful spatial authority begins with temporal territory.
2. One reality, several questions. Present, Day, Week, and Month are resolutions of one life, not modules.
3. The human refers, then establishes. Reference, refinement, and drafting are not canonical truth.
4. Co-occupation stays visible. Overlap does not collapse truth or seal the underlying territory.
5. Silence is not availability. Nothing established, unavailable, available within a legitimate boundary, and incomplete evidence remain distinct.
6. The present locates. It does not decide.
7. Intention survives off the clock. The Active Thread remains recoverable through interruption.
8. Direction sits beside time. Non-temporal Direction is not forced onto coordinates or converted into progress.
9. Identity is allowed. Rank is not. Visual identity may distinguish semantics without manufacturing importance.
10. Focus is a lens, not a world. Context emphasis does not create another reality.
11. Capture and Notes stay off the territory. They remain reachable without becoming the temporal instrument.
12. Motion reports a real change. Animation follows semantic change rather than decoration.
