# Today

Date: 2026-10-02.

## Decision

Today means this Task is deliberately planned for the user's current civil day.

For an open Task, that relationship is `planned_on` equal to the civil date of a supplied instant in the confirmed IANA time zone. Today is not a table, a boolean, a status, or a rank.

Due, Must Do, Active Thread, and the day the Task was created do not establish it. Planning Today does not change those facts, and it does not reserve time.

If no time zone is confirmed, the product does not guess a civil day from the browser and does not write `planned_on`.

## Context

Discovery left "what a day is" unresolved, and described Today as intentional commitment rather than every open Task. V0-005 needs a truthful current day without turning Today into capacity, a Timeline, or NOW.

## Consequences

- `planned_on` remains the only persisted plan.
- Completing a Task removes it from the open Today projection and leaves `planned_on` as historical truth.
- The bottom bar stays Tasks and Schedule.
