# Blocks

Date: 2026-10-02.

## Decision

A Block is time the user has deliberately chosen a purpose for.

Protected Time means: "I have made this time unavailable for allocation."

A Block means: "I have chosen what this time is for."

Those facts are independent. Creating, editing, or removing one does not change the other. They may overlap.

## Context

Protected Time established unavailability. It did not say what a period is for. A Block is that choice. It is not a Task, not Today, not a Work shift, and not a future Commitment.

## Consequences

- `blocks` is its own table. It is not a generic event table and it does not share rows with `protected_time`.
- Purpose is required, at most 80 characters, and written by the user. Context is optional and does not replace purpose. A Block cannot reference another user's Context.
- All-day is a civil date. Timed is local start and end on a civil date, read in the confirmed IANA zone. An end at or before the start continues into the next civil date only.
- A later confirmed-zone change reinterprets existing local times. The zone is not stored on the row.
- Overlaps are kept. Nothing recurs. No Task is stored on a Block. Past rows stay stored and stay off the default Schedule list.
- Timeline and capacity are not calculated.
