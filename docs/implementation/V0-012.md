# V0-012 — Temporal meaning choice

Date: 2026-10-03.

See time, refer to time, then establish what that time means.

V0-011 lets the user refer to a local span. This tranche asks what kind of truth that span is meant to become. The answer is not saved.

## What was verified first

`main` was clean at `5c89b69fae91272c96d0d0dbdeed8168e8a784f5`, matching `origin/main`. Hosted migration history was not changed. The seven migration files remain `20261002213000` through `20261003040000`. Meaning choice does not need a table, a column, or a migration. If it had needed one, implementation would have stopped.

## Phone evidence carried forward

The deployed V0-011 gesture was used on a real phone. A brief hold, a vertical drag, and release select the intended span. An upward selection feels natural. The interaction feels like selecting a portion of the day. That evidence earned the gesture. V0-012 does not replace it.

The same use looked at Manage schedule under the day: Work Schedule, Protected Time, Blocks, and Commitments. Those surfaces may later fold into the canvas, because the user can manipulate the time they are already looking at. That is direction. They stay where they are. Manage schedule becomes less important only when the canvas has earned the ability to perform its responsibilities. This tranche does not promise to remove it.

## Where it lives

The selection session in `components/daySelection.ts` now holds the range and the intention together. The range value is still `{ civilDate, startMinute, endMinute }`. Beside it:

```text
intendedMeaning: "protected_time" | "block" | "commitment" | null
```

That pair is interaction state. It is not a domain primitive, not a Timeline fact, and not a row. `DayCanvas` renders it. `WorkSchedule` does not read it and does not prefill a form from it. `projections/dayCanvas.ts` does not know about it.

## Sequence

A settled selection shows the written range, then:

What does this time mean?

- Protect this time
- Choose a purpose
- Add a commitment

Clear stays with the range. The question is not shown while a drag is still open, and it is not shown when nothing is selected. There is no Continue step and no trip down to Manage schedule.

Choosing one of the three does not write anything. The same range remains, and the session shows the kind:

- Protected time. Unavailable for allocation.
- Block. What this time is for.
- Commitment. An established constraint.

No label, purpose, Context, or title is asked. Change meaning returns to the three actions and keeps the range. Clear removes the range and the intention. A new gesture replaces both, and the new range starts at the question again. A gesture that is cancelled, or a touch that scrolls instead, puts the previous range and the previous intention back.

Previous day, Next day, and Today still remount the session empty when the civil day changes. Opening Manage schedule increments the discard token and remounts it empty. A successful Work-week save, or a successful Protected Time, Block, or Commitment save or remove, still reloads the day and increments that token. A changed confirmed time zone changes it as well. No stale intention survives those.

## What is not a choice

Work is not offered. Neither is a shift, Opening, Mid, or Closing. Work keeps its own schedule semantics.

A Task is not offered. No Task is created, and no Task is associated with a Block.

## Facts, capacity, and DST

The question stays up when the range crosses Work, Protected Time, a Block, a Commitment, or more than one of those. Nothing is disabled. Protected Time does not outrank the other choices. The copy does not say occupied, conflict, available, free, open slot, priority, or recommendation.

The chooser does not claim that the range can hold the chosen kind. Unestablished time is not available time. No capacity is calculated.

The spring-forward sentence and the fall-back sentence stay on the readout while the user chooses. The local minutes are not resolved to an instant.

## Left alone

The hold, the 10px slop, the 220ms touch threshold, 15-minute snapping, the visible-scrollport clamp, and the absence of edge auto-scroll are unchanged. Timed facts are still not pointer targets. The all-day list is still not selectable. Bottom navigation is still Tasks and Schedule.

No migration. No production rows were written. No semantic field was added to a form.

No Capacity, availability, free time, conflict model, priority, NOW, current-time line, month, week, Task composition, Task and Block relationship, recurring obligations, reminders, Pulse, notifications, Google Calendar, Gemini, OAuth, synchronization, voice, watch app, service worker, AI, LLMs, machine learning, or agents.

No dependency was added.

## Limits noticed while building

The question sits under the written range and above the scrolling axis, so the range stays in view while the kind is chosen. Three stacked actions are easy to tap and take vertical space on a phone before the day scroller. That is acceptable for this proof. The intended-state sentences were enough to show which kind was named. A purpose, a title, and a decision about Protected Time's optional label were not needed to understand the handoff, and they were not collected.

That is enough to ask what information is minimally required to establish the truth. It is not enough to create it.

Known current answers, not built here:

- Protected Time. The selected range may be sufficient. Optional labeling already exists and has to be inspected before that form is designed.
- Block. A purpose is required. Context may be optional.
- Commitment. A title is required.
