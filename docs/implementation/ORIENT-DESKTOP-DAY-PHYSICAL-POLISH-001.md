# ORIENT-DESKTOP-DAY-PHYSICAL-POLISH-001

Physical inspection later accepted this Day, including the removed Work Off label and the quieter baseline. [PRODUCTION-DESKTOP-READING-ACCEPTANCE-001.md](PRODUCTION-DESKTOP-READING-ACCEPTANCE-001.md) promotes that Day to `/`.

Physical inspection had already accepted the structure of [ORIENT-DESKTOP-DAY-INSCRIPTION-002.md](ORIENT-DESKTOP-DAY-INSCRIPTION-002.md). This was a small polish on that desktop Day. It was not, at the time it was written, full acceptance, and it did not yet replace `/`.

## Work Off

The word Off on the desktop Day line was a Work schedule state, `entry.state === "off"` for the selected civil date. `DesktopSignature` rendered that label inside the inscription rail, and the rail placed it at the right edge. Timeline does not treat Work Off as occupied time, so the label was not a point on the 00:00–24:00 line.

Desktop Day no longer renders it. Phone Day, Week, Month, and the Exact time clock still do.

## Baseline

The neutral day line stays one 2px stroke in the same warm ink. Its opacity moves from 0.50 to 0.36. Geometry, spans, and Now are unchanged.

## Unchanged

Present, established spans, Now, Week, Month, Direction, Exact time, the drawer, phone, auth, and Day-anchor behavior are unchanged.
