# V0-004A — compact Work schedule

Date: 2026-10-02.

The deployed Work schedule was used on a phone. The stored facts were right. The page was not. Every unknown day showed Shift and Off, so the week was a stack of editors. Reading the schedule and changing it were the same screen.

## Two modes

The default surface answers what the Work schedule is. Edit week answers a request to change it.

Normal view keeps the heading, the confirmed time zone and its Change control, today's stored day, Previous and Next, and the Saturday-through-Friday range. The seven days are a short list:

- a scheduled day shows the local start, the local end, and Opening, Mid, or Closing
- Off shows the word Off
- a date with no row shows an em dash, with the words "Not entered" for assistive technology

Before, during, and after are not repeated on those rows. Home Work orientation owns the current position. Today on this surface is the stored day: no schedule entered, Off, or the shift type and times.

## Edit week

Edit week reveals concise controls on each day. One day can open its start, end, and shift type. The other days stay closed.

A save writes that one day through the existing persistence functions and leaves Edit week open, with the form closed, so the next day can be entered. Done leaves Edit week. A change to start, end, or shift type is kept if the user tries to leave, switch days, or change weeks before saving or canceling. Previous and Next still move exactly seven civil days, and they do not turn Edit week on.

Unknown can become a shift or Off. A shift can be edited, marked Off, or removed. Off can become a shift or be removed. Removing deletes the row, which is unknown again. Off stays a stored row.

## What did not change

No domain type, database table, or migration changed. Unknown is still the absence of a row. Off is still an explicit row. A scheduled day is still one local start, one local end, and an explicit shift type. Overnight shifts, the confirmed IANA zone, and the Saturday-first Work week are unchanged. `projectWorkDay` and `projectWorkOrientation` were not modified. Resume, home Work orientation, Capture, and Open Tasks were not redesigned.

The time zone is still confirmed on this surface. The browser suggestion is still only a suggestion. There is no general settings area.

## Mobile and accessibility

The week is a vertical list, aimed at a narrow phone. Normal view has no per-day editor, so the week is short enough to read at a glance. Edit controls use the existing large tap height. Day controls are named with the civil date. Off, Not entered, and the shift words do not depend on color. A failed save stays on that day. Opening a day moves focus to its start time. Closing it returns focus to that day's shift control.

## Exclusions

V0-005, Today, NOW, `rankNow`, Task ranking, recurring obligations, Notes, Blocks, Commitments, Timeline, reminders, Pulse, Google Calendar, notifications, voice, a service worker, AI, LLMs, machine learning, and agents were not added.
