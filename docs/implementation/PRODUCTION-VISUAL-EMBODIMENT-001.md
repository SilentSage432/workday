# PRODUCTION-VISUAL-EMBODIMENT-001 — Visual correction

Date: 2026-10-05.

Baseline: `67e12b075a1907750bcf1a31666e7a8dff78d240`.

Chassis: [PRODUCTION-UI-001.md](PRODUCTION-UI-001.md). This record does not replace that chassis, and it does not amend domain semantics, schema, or the production-experience design.

## Human rejection

The first physical inspection rejected the visual embodiment. The human's words were:

- "It literally looks like a terrible version of Calendly and that is not how I'm imagining this."
- "Why white? That is the worst color selection ever."
- "None of those things look like buttons. I had to essentially guess that they were."
- "This does not look like a TeamLab production at all."

That skin is not accepted. The interaction and runtime chassis is not rejected.

## What stayed

`/` remains the production instrument. Present, Day, Week, and Month remain questions of one field. Projections, writes, explicit Save, establishment, inspection, the Active Thread, Context as a transient lens, Capture and Notes, Week and Month authority, Direction, Capacity, co-occupation, typed silence, and the phone bezel / desktop dock architecture are unchanged.

## What reopened

Dark field, glass, blur, gradients, luminosity, atmospheric depth, richer material, and typography beyond the system form were available again. They are used only where they carry temporal meaning or make a control operable.

## What changed, and why

The paper ground is gone. The field is a deep continuous environment. Hour marks are margin calibration, not ruled appointment paper. Temporal truths are translucent duration bands with kind-specific edges, so co-occupation stays visible and a fact is not an event card.

Week leads with the shape of established structure across seven dated days. Month leads with that structure as a 28-day landscape. Dates stay locatable. Direction uses a serif, non-temporal material beside the field.

Controls are shaped surfaces. Primary acts and secondary acts differ. Icons accompany the reach controls, the thread, return to Now, and Ask Day / Refer. Now is a luminous presence through the field, and the return control is a filled act.

Capture is two instrument regions: a direct task line, and an expression whose Note and Task authorities are separate controls. Transient surfaces are glass over the field so they read as instruments, not as another region of time. Blur is limited to that instrumentation and the reach layer.

Outfit and Newsreader come from `next/font/google`. Outfit carries the instrument. Newsreader carries Direction, because Direction does not occupy time. No separate font package was added.

Reduced motion still removes animation and transition.

Human visual acceptance is still required. This is not operational adoption.
