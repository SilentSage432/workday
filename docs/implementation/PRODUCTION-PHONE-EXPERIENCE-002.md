# PRODUCTION-PHONE-EXPERIENCE-002 — Day as the phone entry

Physical inspection at 412 × 924 accepted the phone-native architecture. The same inspection showed that phone Day, not phone Present, is the orientation a person wants when Orient opens.

The meanings did not swap. The experiential roles did.

Day remains a question over a selected civil date. On the phone it is the orientation surface: the date, Now as a secondary locative mark when that date is today, the day's established facts in clock order, the whole-day signature, the Active Thread, and Exact time.

Present remains the immediate-moment resolution: large authoritative time, every truth containing Now, and the Active Thread beside those truths because they may disagree. It is not the temporal editor.

Exact time remains precision depth. It opens the existing vertical Day clock. It is not a fifth question and it is not Present.

Week remains shape across bounded days. Month remains the broader 7 × 4 landscape with Direction. A quiet Month date asks Day for that civil date, not for authoritative today.

The phone distance is semantic resolution. Month, Week, Day, Present move closer to lived time. Present, Day, Week, Month move farther out to structure. The question control still says Present, Day, Week, and Month. It does not say Home, Today, Agenda, Now Mode, or Overview.

## Fresh phone entry

A fresh phone instrument asks Day. The anchor is the authoritative civil date the instrument already derives from the confirmed timezone. That rule runs only before the human has chosen a question in the open instrument. It is not stored. A later resize does not take the question back.

A clock tick updates Now. It does not change the question or a selected Day anchor. A Day that was moved to another civil date stays there. If that date is not today, Now is not drawn inside it.

Choosing Present moves to authoritative Now. Choosing Day again restores the Day anchor remembered for that question. After Month asks October 8, Present shows October 5's instant, and Day returns to October 8.

Desktop still opens on Present. Shared ontology does not require the same starting composition.
