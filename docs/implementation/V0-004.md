# V0-004 — Work temporal orientation

Date: 2026-10-02.

V0-004 teaches the deterministic projection layer how established Work facts relate to a supplied instant. It answers where the user is in the Work day. It does not decide what they should do.

## Deterministic intelligence

The product's runtime intelligence is deterministic. That constraint is recorded in [../decisions/2026-10-02-deterministic-intelligence.md](../decisions/2026-10-02-deterministic-intelligence.md). This tranche did not add a model, a ranking function, or a probabilistic interpreter.

## The three Work clocks

The clocks stay distinct.

**Shift clock.** Where the supplied instant sits relative to the operative scheduled shift: before, during, or after. V0-004 composes `projectWorkDay`. It does not invent lunch, afternoon, or a final hour.

**Store clock.** Operational periods that exist whether or not the user is on shift. The executable period is Power Hour, 10:00–14:00 local, half-open, on the civil date of the supplied instant.

**Week clock.** The Saturday-first Lowe's fiscal week remains `workFiscalWeekStart`. Work orientation does not recompute it, and an overnight shift does not move it.

## Work cadence representation

Established constants live in `domain/workCadence.ts`.

- Power Hour is a named Window: 10:00 through 14:00 local.
- Full Shelf Replenishment has one executable timing fact: intended before 10:00 local.
- Opening step names are recorded so the sequence is not lost. The projection does not select a current step.

There is no cadence table, no editor, and no rule engine. The constants are product truth.

## Work orientation

`projectWorkOrientation` takes a supplied instant, a confirmed IANA time zone, today's schedule entry, and the previous day's entry. A missing entry is unknown. It returns facts:

- work date
- schedule state, and when scheduled: shift type, local bounds, whether the shift continues after midnight, position, and the civil date the shift was entered on
- Power Hour state: before, during, or after
- the next established boundary, or none
- a cadence fact: none, opening with the FSR boundary still ahead or reached, mid, or closing

The home screen maps those facts into a few lines. It does not recompute them.

The operative shift is today's scheduled shift, unless yesterday's overnight shift still contains the instant and today's shift does not. If both contain the instant, today's later start is operative. That tie is chronological. It is not an importance ranking.

## Power Hour

States are before, during, and after. The next boundary can be Power Hour beginning or ending.

During Power Hour the projection does not create a Task, clear the Active Thread, complete anything, or rank Tasks. The home line says "Power Hour" when that state is current and a shift is scheduled. It does not tell the user to stop their thread.

## FSR

On an Opening shift, while that shift contains the instant, the projection says whether the instant is still before 10:00 on the shift's scheduled civil date. The home shows "FSR is intended before 10:00 AM" only while that boundary is still ahead.

The approximate 11:00 expectation is not encoded. Passing 10:00 does not mean FSR is unfinished, late, or overdue. No FSR Task is created.

## Opening, Mid, and Closing

During an Opening shift, the only clock-relative cadence fact is the FSR boundary above. The rest of the opening sequence stays sequence-relative and is not rendered as a checklist.

During a Mid shift, the cadence fact is that the shift is Mid. No Mid sequence is invented. Power Hour remains a store-clock fact.

During a Closing shift, the cadence fact is that the shift is Closing. Closing concerns stay in [../../CADENCE.md](../../CADENCE.md). They are not projected as this user's sequence, and cash office and perimeter duties are not assigned.

No "later portion" of a Closing shift is projected. No clock boundary for that portion was established.

Before a shift, after a shift, Off, and unknown all carry cadence kind `none`.

## Weekly obligations

Bay Audits, Cycle Counts, the Monday–Thursday runway, and Thursday department readiness are not projected. Saying they are due, preferred, or unfinished would require occurrence and completion truth this tranche does not have. The week clock remains the existing fiscal-week function only.

## Next boundary

The next boundary is the earliest established instant strictly after the supplied instant. Candidates are the operative shift's start and end, and today's Power Hour start and end. If two instants are equal, the stable order is shift start, Power Hour start, Power Hour end, shift end. That order is a tie-break, not importance.

After the shift and after Power Hour on that civil date, the next boundary is none. Tomorrow's shift is not consulted. Cadence times that were never established are not candidates.

On an Off or unknown day the projection can still name the next Power Hour boundary, because the store clock does not depend on the user's shift. The home does not show it. Off is one quiet line. Unknown is "No Work schedule entered."

## Overnight shifts and time zones

An overnight shift stays attached to the civil date it was entered on. Power Hour stays attached to the civil date of the instant. The fiscal week is computed from that instant and the confirmed zone, not from the shift's start date.

Local bounds use the existing IANA conversion. Invalid local times are rejected. The spring-forward test keeps Power Hour's start at 10:00 local.

## Active Thread and Tasks

Resume still answers what the user was doing. Work orientation answers where they are in the Work day.

The projection does not receive Tasks or the Active Thread. It does not start, clear, or replace a thread. It does not create cadence Tasks, set MUST DO, change Planned or Due, complete Tasks, filter them, or rank them.

## Home

When a time zone is confirmed, the signed-in home shows Work after Resume and before Capture. Resume stays the visible thread. Capture stays collapsed. Open Tasks stay an inventory in creation order.

If the schedule read fails, the home says it could not load the schedule. It does not show Off.

No confirmed time zone means the block is omitted. The product does not infer a zone.

## Database

No migration. No new durable user truth was required. Power Hour and the FSR boundary are constants, not rows.

Real user rows were not mutated. At the start of this tranche the dedicated project still had one Task, one Active Thread, no confirmed time zone, and no schedule rows.

## Tests

`projections/workOrientation.test.ts` covers shift position, Off, unknown, Opening, Mid, Closing, Power Hour states, the next boundary through the day, the empty boundary after the shift, an untouched Task and Active Thread, an overnight shift, a timezone change, the Saturday-first week, the spring-forward 10:00 boundary, and the absence of a hidden clock or network call.

## Exclusions

NOW, `rankNow`, Task ranking, inferred urgency, automatic cadence Tasks, recurring-obligation persistence, Notes, Blocks, Commitments, Timeline, reminders, Pulse, Google Calendar, notifications, voice, a service worker, AI, LLMs, machine learning, and agents were not added.
