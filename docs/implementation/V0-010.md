# V0-010 — Day temporal canvas

Date: 2026-10-02.

Time is the canvas.

Timeline composes temporal truth. It does not resolve temporal truth.

Nothing established is not the same as available.

This tranche draws one selected civil day from the Timeline projection. It is the first visual resolution. Month, week, and NOW are not built.

## Why day is first

A day is where temporal geometry becomes concrete: when something begins, when it ends, how long it lasts, what overlaps, what covers the whole day, and what part of an overnight fact continues here. The user should see that shape instead of translating separate lists.

The day is also the resolution that should later accept manipulation. V0-010 does not implement that. It proves the read-only grammar first.

Likely later interactions, not approved here: select or drag a time range, establish Protected Time, a Block, or a Commitment, inspect or edit existing truth, move or resize a deliberate allocation, drag a Task into time, and possibly associate a Task with a Block.

## Route

Bottom navigation stays Tasks and Schedule. `/schedule` leads with the day canvas. "Manage schedule" reveals the existing Work week, Protected Time, Blocks, and Commitments. The confirmed time zone stays on the page, using the same confirmation control. No third destination and no new settings surface were added.

The selected day is local state. It starts as the civil date of the supplied instant in the confirmed zone. Previous day and Next day step by civil date, using Lucide chevrons. Today returns to that confirmed civil date. Nothing about the selection is stored.

Without a confirmed zone, the canvas does not position the day.

## Data

`composeDayCanvas` calls `projectTimeline` for `[D, D + 1)`. The component does not recompute which facts exist.

Look-behind: timed truth continues at most into the next civil date. `dayCanvasWorkQuery` asks `loadWorkSchedule` for the previous civil date through `D`, inclusive. `loadProtectedTime`, `loadBlocks`, and `loadCommitments` return every stored row, so the previous day is included. Those arrays are not filtered to `D` before Timeline.

A successful Work-week save, or a successful Protected Time, Block, or Commitment save or remove, reloads the canvas. The canvas itself does not write.

## Geometry

The timed axis is 24 local clock hours, midnight through the next midnight. Each hour is one row. Position and height come from the Timeline `intersection`, converted to local minutes on the selected day and divided by 1440 clock minutes. That divisor is the clock face, not a claim that the civil day lasts 24 elapsed hours. Source bounds remain on the label. `clipped` is true when the intersection differs from the source bounds, which is how an overnight tail stays honest about its start.

All-day facts sit above the axis. They are not fake timed cards.

Unresolved timed facts sit in "Unresolved time", with the sentence "Time could not be positioned for this date." Stored local text remains. No hour is invented. The sentence is not an error.

If the two civil midnights are not 24 elapsed hours apart, the canvas says placement uses local clock labels. A resolved 1:00–3:00 span on a spring-forward morning occupies two clock hours. The missing hour is not filled in. A fall-back repeated hour is drawn once, using Timeline's first round-trip. If the midnights themselves cannot be resolved, the timed axis is omitted.

Empty space is unlabeled. The canvas does not calculate gaps or call them free, available, open, or capacity. There is no current-time line.

A timed fact may be drawn at least `1.75rem` tall so a very short span can still be identified. `data-minutes` is the true duration. The minimum is display only.

## Visual treatment

The treatments are provisional and meant to be revised from use.

Work is a full-width wash with a left border. It reads as broad context, not as the loudest card. Protected Time is a full-width dashed hatch painted over that wash, so both can remain visible. Blocks and Commitments are foreground facts. Overlapping foreground facts divide width into lanes. The lowest free lane is used. A boundary that only touches is not overlap. Equal starts break the tie by source id. That tie-break is stability, not importance. Lane count is per overlap cluster, so a fact that overlaps nothing keeps the foreground width.

No badge calls overlap a conflict. Kind is not priority. Each fact carries its own words: Work and shift type, Protected and its label, Block and purpose, Commitment and title. A Block context name can appear quietly. Commitment origin stays off the card. Accessible names include the kind, the label, the source interval, and the shown interval when the geometry is clipped.

## Limits noticed while building

The timed axis is the full civil day, inside its own scroll that starts at midnight. The page does not jump to a working-hour region. Dense overlap becomes narrow lanes, and the display minimum can visually crowd a short fact without changing its recorded minutes. Fall-back still has one clock position for a repeated hour. Work as a wash and Protected Time as a hatch are a first guess: both stayed perceptible beside a Block, and neither yet feels like the final treatment. That is enough evidence to design direct time selection next, and not enough to freeze the visual hierarchy.
