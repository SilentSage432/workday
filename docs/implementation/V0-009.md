# V0-009 — Timeline composition

Date: 2026-10-02.

Timeline composes temporal truth. It does not resolve temporal truth.

Time is the underlying space. Multiple established truths may describe the same time without one canceling the others.

Nothing established is not the same as available.

This tranche adds that composition as a pure projection over Work schedule entries, Protected Time, Blocks, and Commitments. It does not add a screen.

## Why it exists

Those four facts were stored separately. That remains correct. A later day, week, or month surface needs one place that can say which of them meet a requested civil period, without flattening their meanings or treating unmarked time as free.

The durable rules are in [../decisions/2026-10-02-timeline-composition.md](../decisions/2026-10-02-timeline-composition.md).

## Projection

`projectTimeline` takes:

- `range`: `{ startsOn, endsBefore }`, a half-open civil range
- `timeZone`: the confirmed IANA zone
- `workSchedule`, `protectedTime`, `blocks`, and `commitments`: the rows the caller already loaded

It returns `TimelineFact` values. It does not read `Date.now`, the network, or Supabase. It does not write. The same inputs return the same facts.

`startsOn` is included. `endsBefore` is excluded. A fact that begins before the range is included when its interval meets the range. A fact that begins on `endsBefore` is not.

Timed truth in this product continues into the next civil date at most. Look-behind is that one previous civil day. The function does not query for it. A caller that wants the Saturday portion of a Friday night shift passes the Friday row.

## Facts

Each fact keeps its semantic source:

- `work_schedule`, with shift type and civil-date identity
- `protected_time`, with its label when one was stored
- `block`, with purpose and optional Context id
- `commitment`, with title and `origin`

`origin` is provenance. `sourceKind` is not. A user-created Commitment is still `user_created`.

All-day facts stay all-day. Their inclusion is `{ status: "civil" }`. They are not turned into a 00:00–24:00 interval.

Timed facts keep local start, local end, owning civil date, and whether the end is read on the next civil date. When both ends resolve, `bounds` is the full source interval and `intersection` is the portion inside the range. An overnight shift requested for Saturday still says it started Friday.

When a local time does not occur, both `bounds` and `intersection` are unresolved. The fact remains if its claimed civil span meets the range. A repeated local time uses the existing first round-trip and keeps its clock text.

Work Off produces no fact. A civil date with no Work row produces no fact. Neither means the day is available.

## Overlap and order

Overlapping facts stay separate. Protected Time is not split or released around a Block. The projection has no conflict, winner, rank, or capacity result.

Order follows the owning civil date, then all-day before timed, then local start, then source kind as a tie-break, then source id. That order is not importance. A Friday-owned overnight fact sorts by Friday.

## Future evidence

The later temporal surface is an editor. The day resolution can select time and establish what it means. The week resolution was described here as where the week is spoken for, where a purpose was chosen, and where nothing is established. [../decisions/2026-10-05-week-contract.md](../decisions/2026-10-05-week-contract.md) qualifies that wording. "Spoken for" is not a domain category. Week perceives where established temporal structure exists, what kind it is, and where nothing has been established. The month resolution was called orientation here. [../decisions/2026-10-05-month-contract.md](../decisions/2026-10-05-month-contract.md) decides the question. NOW is where the user is inside that truth. None of those are built here.

A person may later drag a Task into time. The Task would remain a Task, and the allocation would be a Block associated with it. That is not a stored relationship in V0-009.

Planned Tasks, reminders, occurrences, and Windows are not Timeline inputs yet.

## Left alone

No migration was added. Hosted migration history still matches the seven local files, from `20261002213000` through `20261003040000`. This tranche did not write production rows.

Task, Today, Must Do, Due, Active Thread, Resume, the Work schedule, Protected Time, Blocks, Commitments, and the confirmed time zone are unchanged as stored truth. Their existing list projections are unchanged.

Google Calendar, Gemini, OAuth, synchronization, capacity, availability, free time, a conflict model, source precedence, Task composition, a Task and Block association, recurring obligations, NOW, `rankNow`, reminders, Pulse, notifications, voice, a watch app, a service worker, AI, LLMs, machine learning, agents, and a visual calendar were not added.

## Tests

`projections/timeline.test.ts` covers the four sources, Off and unknown Work, overlap, the half-open range, overnight clipping, a midnight end that does not occupy the next date, a missing local hour, a repeated local hour, all-day truth on a spring-forward date, order, unchanged inputs, and the absence of a clock or another projection.
