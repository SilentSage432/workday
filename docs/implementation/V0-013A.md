# V0-013A — Established truth must not capture time

Date: 2026-10-03.

Baseline: `bdfd1ebcd8a65c9619d704500e36d7afcae68b3d` on `main`, matching `origin/main`.

A rendered fact is something true about temporal territory. It does not own that territory so completely that the user can no longer refer to the time.

## Phone evidence

V0-013 was used on a real phone. Selecting a range, choosing Protected Time, and saving all worked. The contextual surface closed, the transient selection disappeared, and the hatched fact stayed on the day.

The next touch on that rendered Protected region did not start a new temporal selection. Before the save, territory that already contained established facts could be selected and given more meaning. After the save, the painted region blocked the hand from referring to that time.

Domain overlap and canvas reachability are separate. The persistence and domain layer already allowed Work, Protected Time, Blocks, and Commitments to overlap. The canvas is what made the painted territory unreachable.

## What was verified first

The working tree was clean at the baseline. No migration was added. `DOMAIN.md` and `TIME_MODEL.md` were read and left unchanged. Overlap semantics were not changed.

The timed column is the selection listener. Work, Protected Time, Blocks, and Commitments are `<article>` elements inside that column, absolutely positioned, at `z-0`, `z-[1]`, and `z-10`. Since V0-011 those articles have carried `pointer-events: none`, on the assumption that a touch would fall through to the column. The selection reducer does not know about facts, and it does not reject a range because one is already there.

The interaction tests dispatched `pointerdown` on the column. That bypasses hit-testing, so they could pass while a finger on the painted article never entered the listener.

V0-013's outside dismissal was inspected. It runs only while a settled selection exists: a press inside that selection does not start a replacement, and a touch that scrolls does not abandon it. A successful Save remounts the session empty. That path is not active when nothing is selected, so it is not the cause of the post-save failure.

The cause is the split between paint and listener. The articles are the topmost boxes in their territory. `pointer-events: none` was the only instruction to skip them. On the phone, a gesture that began on the rendered Protected article did not enter the selection state machine. The articles also use `overflow: hidden`. This repository cannot see the phone's hit target. It can see that reachability depended on fall-through from those articles to a listener underneath them, and that fall-through did not deliver the gesture.

## Correction

The time column now ends with a transparent layer, `z-[21]`, `touch-action: pan-y`, `pointer-events: auto`. That layer is the timed surface: it receives the pointer, it is the capture element, and the non-passive `touchmove` listener is attached to it. It sits above Work, Protected Time, Blocks, Commitments, and the selection band. It is below the contextual surface, which stays `z-30`. The column is `isolation: isolate`, so this layer's stacking stays inside the column.

The painted articles keep `pointer-events: none`. That remains useful so they are not a second target, and so their accessible names stay in the tree without becoming controls. Reachability no longer depends on the phone honoring that fall-through, because the surface is stacked above the paint.

A normal scroll that begins on a fact still scrolls. The layer uses the same `pan-y` behavior the column used. Movement past 10px before 220ms is still a scroll. `preventDefault` still runs only after selection has begun.

## What this does not decide

Existing-fact interaction stays unresolved. The layer receives the pointer everywhere in the column, including where a fact is painted. A later gesture that talks about the fact has to be recognized there, or this layer has to stop covering that fact. Taking `pointer-events: none` off an article would also have to be reconsidered if the article itself should become the target. Neither choice is a domain rule.

No fact editor, inspector, selection mode, delete, move, or resize was added. Resize handles stay deferred. Overlap is not a conflict, a capacity, or an availability. Unestablished time is not available. Established time is not unreachable.

## Preserved

The 220ms touch hold, the 10px slop, pan before selection, `preventDefault` only after selection begins, the 15-minute tap, 15-minute gesture snapping, upward and downward drags, no auto-scroll, and same-civil-day selection are unchanged.

Close, Clear, and outside dismissal behave as in V0-013. A scroll that begins over a fact does not abandon an open draft. Save is still the authority boundary. The transient band still draws over the facts, and the facts stay visible under it.

## Tests

The canvas tests start the gesture on the timed surface at minutes covered by Work, Protected Time, a Block, a Commitment, and a Block overlapping a Commitment. They cover tap, hold and drag, an upward drag, a downward drag, the hold and the slop, and a scroll that neither selects nor drops an unrelated draft. They also check that the articles stay read-only: no control inside them, and their labels and minutes unchanged.

Authenticated browser validation was not available. The schedule page asks for a sign-in. This tranche does not request credentials or weaken that gate. No production row was created. The phone remains the acceptance test.
