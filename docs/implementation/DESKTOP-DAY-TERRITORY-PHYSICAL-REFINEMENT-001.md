# DESKTOP-DAY-TERRITORY-PHYSICAL-REFINEMENT-001

Date: 2026-10-08.

Prior tranche: [DESKTOP-DAY-SPATIAL-TERRITORY-001.md](DESKTOP-DAY-SPATIAL-TERRITORY-001.md).

## Purpose

Material refinement of ordinary desktop Day after production physical observation.

This tranche does **not** redesign the validated spatial model.

## Physical evidence that validated the spatial model

- Horizontal 00:00–24:00 temporal territory works
- Day scale / claimed stage is directionally correct
- Duration-derived Work extent works
- Now as gold locative coordinate works
- Temporal silence works

## Physical defects observed

1. The bounded plane read as a giant empty card/container.
2. Work territory read as a large translucent calendar appointment.
3. Membership inscriptions and their temporal territories felt visually disconnected.
4. Now behavior must be preserved (success).

## De-containerization law

Day territory remains perceptibly bounded by axis, landmarks, occupation, Now, and a restrained open field.

It must **not** be established primarily by a conventional enclosing card rectangle.

Implemented as:

- remove full rectangular border / radius / inset card chrome
- open atmospheric field wash
- faded top and bottom structural lines (axis grammar, not a box)

## Temporal-territory vs card distinction

Occupation marks use band material (`data-occupation-material="band"`):

- soft vertical fade wash
- start/end edge emphasis (duration boundaries)
- no rounded event-card fill

Duration-derived horizontal extent is unchanged. Size still does not imply importance.

## Membership ↔ territory correspondence law

Membership names WHAT / KIND / INTERVAL. Territory shows WHEN / HOW LONG / RELATIONSHIP.

Correspondence is carried by shared semantic material:

- kind hue family
- contour grammar
- restrained membership wash in the same hue family as the territory band

No connector lines, legends, duplicated titles inside territories, or hover-only meaning.

## Now preserved

Gold vertical Now coordinate through the territory is unchanged in semantics and general behavior. Locative only.

## Silence preserved

Unoccupied territory remains unmarked. No free/available/grid/filler treatment.

## Geometry intentionally frozen

- Day stage allocation
- plane height clamps/caps
- Work start/end placement and widths
- landmarks
- Exact Time relationship
- all-day outside timed plane
- Active Thread placement
- LOOK · ADD · ACT borrow law
