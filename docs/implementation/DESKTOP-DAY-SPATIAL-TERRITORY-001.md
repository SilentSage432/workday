# DESKTOP-DAY-SPATIAL-TERRITORY-001

Date: 2026-10-08.

Baseline HEAD: `1e257e78827cca7f2e0a08d87c9363a8b90938e1` (`main`).

Prior discovery: DESKTOP-SPATIAL-EXPERIENCE-DISCOVERY-001 (Direction 3 — Relational Temporal Territory).

## Purpose

Transform ordinary desktop Day from a thin temporal inscription above an accidentally unused stage into Orient’s first bounded **relational temporal territory**.

This tranche owns only the resting desktop Day reading.

It is not:

- a calendar redesign
- Exact Time / DayClock
- a phone clone
- a dashboard
- revival of the rejected vertical Day field (`ORIENT-DESKTOP-DAY-FIELD-001`)

## Spatial law

- Desktop Day claims meaningful vertical stage for a **bounded horizontal** 00:00–24:00 plane.
- Duration may earn spatial extent.
- Spatial size must not imply importance.
- Unmarked territory is silence, not availability.
- Persistent space remains earned by semantic relationship.

## Duration → extent / size ≠ importance

Timed Work, Protected Time, Block, Commitment, and external timed observations keep horizontal placement from `signaturePlacement` / day-canvas minutes. Wider marks mean longer duration only. Kind identity stays in hue, contour, and material — not rank.

## Now boundary

When the selected Day is today, Now remains a gold locative coordinate through the territory (`data-day-now-coordinate`). It does not become urgency, countdown, judgment, or progress. Non-today Day omits Now.

## Silence boundary

Unoccupied plane territory is unmarked. The reading does not label it free, available, open, unused, or capacity.

## Exact Time boundary

Ordinary Day shows the shape of temporal truth. Exact Time remains the precision doorway into the existing Day clock. Empty-territory pointer behavior still enters Exact Time / proposal flow. No minute editing, dense handles, capacity paint, or DayClock behavior on ordinary Day.

## External provenance boundary

External timed observations may occupy duration-derived extent with an observed material (`data-observed="true"`) that does not imply human adoption. They do not gain DTM or edit authority. Full provenance remains on inspection.

## All-day

All-day facts stay membership truth outside the timed plane. They are never painted as fake 24-hour timed appointments.

## Membership

Membership labels continue to name kind / title / interval. The territory communicates when / how long / coexistence. Active Thread remains off-clock above the plane.

## Phone unchanged

Phone Day geometry, membership cards, signature, and LOOK · ADD · ACT bezel are untouched. Territory attributes are desktop-scoped.
